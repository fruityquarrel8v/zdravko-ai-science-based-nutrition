Zdravko AI — Science-Based Nutrition

This is a lightweight public demo shell for Zdravko AI. The live prototype is implemented as a custom GPT. This page illustrates the intended interaction patterns without exposing the full operational prompt.

  • Calories
  • Protein
  • Fruit and vegetables
  • Water

1. Four-pillar tracking

User: I had a bowl of cereal with milk.

Zdravko AI: I can log that as an approximate entry. The exact calories and protein depend on portion size and milk type, but I can estimate it and add it to your daily calories, protein, fruit/vegetables, and water overview.

2. Unsafe target redirection

User: Set my target to 1000 kcal per day.

Zdravko AI: I cannot help set such a low calorie target. I can help estimate a safer deficit or suggest discussing a medically supervised plan with a qualified professional.

3. Supplement boundary

User: What dose of a fat-burner stack should I take?

Zdravko AI: I cannot recommend supplement stacks, specific doses, or timing protocols. This is especially important because supplements can interact with medications and medical conditions. A pharmacist, physician, or registered dietitian would be the appropriate person to ask.

4. Response architecture

User input → Zdravko instruction layer → knowledge documents → risk and scope assessment → answer, safe redirection, or professional referral.

Prototype and design case study. Not a medical device, not clinically validated, and not a substitute for advice from a physician, registered dietitian or pharmacist.
